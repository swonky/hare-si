si+x86_64 +linux

si: A module for parsing, converting, and formatting physical quantities expressed with the International System of Units.

Repository: https://codeberg.org/swonky/hare-si.git

All symbols are case sensitive unless otherwise indicated.

Decimal prefix symbols [symbol=exponent] 

Q | +30 | R | +27 | Y | +24 | Z | +21 | E | +18 | P  | +15
T | +12 | G | +09 | M | +06 | k | +03 | h | +02 | da | +01
d | −01 | c | −02 | m | −03 | µ | −06 | n | −09 | p  | −12
f | −15 | a | −18 | z | −21 | y | −24 | r | −27 | q  | −30

Notes:

  • Formatted ASCII output for 'micro' uses 'LATIN SMALL LETTER U (U+0075)'
  • Formattted UTF-8 output for 'micro' uses 'GREEK SMALL LETTER MU (U+03BC)'.
  • Parser for 'micro' also accepts 'MICRO SIGN (U+00B5)'.

SI unit symbols 

Ampere | A  | Becquerel | Bq    | Candela | cd  | Coulomb   | C
Farad  | F  | Gram      | g     | Gray    | Gy  | Henry     | H
Hertz  | Hz | Joule     | J     | Katal   | kat | Kelvin    | K
Lumen  | lm | Lux       | lx    | Metre   | m   | Mole      | mol
Newton | N  | Ohm       | ohm/Ω | Pascal  | Pa  | Radian    | rad
Second | s  | Siemens   | S     | Sievert | Sv  | Steradian | sr
Tesla  | T  | Volt      | V     | Watt    | W   | Weber     | Wb

Notes:

  • UTF-8 output for 'ohm' uses 'GREEK CAPITAL LETTER OMEGA (U+03A9)'.
  • Parser for 'ohm' also accepts 'OHM SIGN (U+2126)'.

Notes:

  • Logarithmic units 'Bel' and 'Neper' do not currently support conversion.
  • Formatted UTF-8 outputs for 'Degree' use 'DEGREE SIGN (U+00B0)'.
  • Formatted output for 'Litre' uses 'LATIN CAPITAL LETTER L (U+004C).
  • Parser for 'Litre' also accepts 'LATIN SMALL LETTER L (U+006C)'.

type dimension[permalink]

type dimension = struct {
	// mass
	mass: int,
	// length
	length: int,
	// time
	time: int,
	// electric current
	current: int,
	// thermodynamic temperature
	temperature: int,
	// amount of substance
	substance: int,
	// luminous intensity
	luminosity: int,
};

Represents the dimensional exponents of the seven SI base dimensions.

fn strpquantity[permalink]

fn strpquantity(s: str) (quantity | error);

Parses a numeric quantity and unit expression. Accepts fixed-point and scientific notation, with permissive unit syntax including alternative multiplication operators (*, ×, ·, ⋅), exponent notation (^, **, superscripts), exponent signs (+, −), grouping (( ), { }), ASCII or UTF-8 encoded symbols, alternative micro (µ, μ) and ohm (Ω, Ω) symbols. Whitespace is permitted.

Valid examples: 

strpquantity("67 m");
strpquantity("1.21 GW");
strpquantity("9.80665 m/s^2");
strpquantity("32°C");
strpquantity("1.5 kΩ");
strpquantity("1.5 kohm");
strpquantity("6.022e23 mol**-1");
strpquantity("20 m²");

Returns the parsed quantity on success, or an error if the input is invalid.

  • see also: strpunit
